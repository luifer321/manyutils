Contact ManyUtils

We're a small team. We read everything you send us, and we usually reply within two business days.

Email us

For all enquiries — bug reports, feature requests, advertising, partnerships, press, or general feedback — send an email to:

contact@manyutils.com

What to include

To help us reply faster, please include:

Bug report

The tool URL, what you tried to do, what happened, what you expected, and (if you can) your browser and OS version.

Feature or new-tool request

A short description of the use case. Real workflows beat abstract feature lists every time.

Advertising / partnerships

Your company, what you'd like to promote, and your monthly budget range.

Press / interviews

Outlet, deadline, and a few sample questions or topics.

Frequently asked

Do you offer paid support or custom tools?

For custom internal tools, white-label utilities, or sponsored tools, email us at the address above with the brief.

Can I use ManyUtils tools on my own site?

The output of any tool (formatted JSON, generated passwords, gradients, palettes, QR codes, etc.) is yours to use freely, including commercially. Embedding the tools themselves into your own site is not currently supported — please get in touch if that's something you need.

How do I report a privacy or security issue?

Please email us with "Security" in the subject line. We treat security reports with the highest priority.

Do you reply in languages other than English?

Yes — feel free to write in English, French, Spanish, German or Portuguese. We'll reply in the same language.
