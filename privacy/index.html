Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

The short version: ManyUtils tools run entirely in your browser. We do not store or transmit any data you enter into our tools. We use standard analytics and advertising services described below.

1 Who We Are

ManyUtils (manyutils.com) provides free browser-based online tools for developers, designers, and general users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit our website.

2 Data We Collect

Tool input data

All processing happens locally in your browser. Data you paste or upload into any ManyUtils tool is never sent to our servers and is not stored or logged in any form.

Automatically collected data

When you visit ManyUtils, standard web server logs and analytics services may automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps. This data is used in aggregate to understand how people use the site and to improve it.

User preferences

We store your language preference in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

3 Cookies

ManyUtils itself does not set first-party cookies. However, third-party services we use (analytics and advertising, described below) may set cookies on your device. These cookies help those services function and measure usage.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some third-party features on the site.

Cookie Provider Purpose Type
Google Analytics Usage analytics Analytics
Google AdSense Advertising Marketing

4 Analytics

We may use analytics services such as Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymised information about how visitors interact with ManyUtils. This helps us understand which tools are most used, where visitors come from, and how to improve the site.

Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, session duration, device type, and geographic region at an approximate level. No personally identifiable information is shared with us through Google Analytics.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5 Advertising (Google AdSense / AdMob)

ManyUtils is supported by advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to ManyUtils and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

6 Third-Party Services

ManyUtils uses a small number of external services to operate. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts — loads web fonts from Google's CDN. Google may log the request. Privacy Policy
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — loads CSS from a CDN. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertising network. Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

7 Data Retention

Because ManyUtils does not collect personal data directly, we have no personal data to retain. Analytics data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own data retention policies. You can review these at policies.google.com.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA) including:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (California residents)

Since we do not collect personal data directly, most of these rights would apply to data held by our third-party service providers. We will do our best to assist you with any requests and will direct you to the appropriate provider where applicable.

9 Children's Privacy

ManyUtils is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
